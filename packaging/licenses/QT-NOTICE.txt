Lguard's desktop app (package lguard-gui) uses Qt 6.8.3 and Qt for Python
(PySide6 / Shiboken6) 6.8.3, unmodified, under the GNU Lesser General Public
License version 3 (LGPL-3.0.txt, which builds on GPL-3.0.txt, both included).

The Qt libraries are separate shared objects in /opt/lguard/gui/site-packages
(PySide6/Qt/lib, shiboken6). You may replace them with your own builds of the
same or a compatible version; Lguard only reports such a change and never
refuses to run because of it.

Complete corresponding source code:
  Qt 6.8.3      https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.8/6.8.3/single/qt-everywhere-src-6.8.3.tar.xz
  PySide6 6.8.3 https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.8.3-src/pyside-setup-everywhere-src-6.8.3.tar.xz
A copy is also available from the Lguard project on request for three years
from the release of this package.

Only LGPL-licensed Qt modules are used (QtCore, QtGui, QtWidgets, QtDBus,
QtSvg); no GPL-only Qt add-on (e.g. Qt Charts) is shipped.
